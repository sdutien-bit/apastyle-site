Student research project

How well do students know APA Style?

We are studying how confidently and how accurately 50 undergraduate students use APA 7th edition in-text citations and reference lists, and how, in their own words, academic writing has changed the way they think critically.

Why this matters

01

Referencing is a core academic skill

Correct citations give credit to other people's ideas, help readers find sources, and show the evidence behind an argument. Errors can look like plagiarism even when they are not.

02

Rules change between editions

APA 7 (2020) changed several rules, for example "et al." from the first citation for three or more authors, and no publisher location for books. Students often mix editions.

03

Writing trains thinking

Choosing sources, weighing evidence and building an argument are critical thinking in action. We ask students how writing has shaped these skills.
